Most value exercises fail because they force you to pick arbitrarily from a disconnected list. This journey combines the world's most effective psychological frameworks into a single, cohesive narrative to help you find your absolute core.

1

Acceptance & Commitment

We start by utilizing the ACT therapy "Card Sort" method. This forces essential cognitive defusion to clearly separate what is fundamentally you versus what is just nice to have.

2

The Meaning Movement

Leveraging a comprehensive taxonomy of values, we cast a wide net and then execute conflict prioritization (the "Conflict Test") to isolate your bedrock Core 5 principles.

3

Dare to Lead

Finally, inspired by Brené Brown's research, we translate these abstract values into actual, lived verbs. You will define the specific actions that support your values and pinpoint the exact warning signs of misalignment.
